### Section 38: Dynamic Isometric Dimension Expansion
Isometric embedding upon codebook capacity saturation:

```math
\iota: \mathcal{H}^{d-1} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{H}^{d+k-1}, \quad \boldsymbol{\mu} \mapsto (\mu_0, \dots, \mu_{d-1}, \underbrace{0, \dots, 0}_{k})^\top
```

```math
\langle \iota(x), \iota(y) \rangle_{\mathcal{L}^{d+k}} \equiv \langle x, y \rangle_{\mathcal{L}^d} = -1.0
```

The embedding preserves inner products. The proposed capacity scaling $`e^{k \cdot R}`$ is a conjectural design claim, not derived here or implemented in the runtime.
